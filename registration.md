# Registration gate

The review console shows the best 10–20 candidates. It must expose evidence without forcing the reviewer to open many tabs.

## Candidate card

Show:

- domain and parsed meaning
- final score and confidence
- predicted keyword clusters
- city, metro, and state search volume
- estimated monthly lead value
- CPC and commercial intent
- top referring domains
- archive timeline
- trademark result
- portfolio overlap
- intended site or redirect use
- AI strengths and risks
- final availability status

## Controls

- maximum three normal registrations per day
- absolute maximum five
- maximum one domain per exact geo/service pair
- maximum two domains in the same service category per day
- require a build, redirect, or hold thesis
- require final registrar availability check
- record approver, evidence version, and reason

Do not automate registration until the system has at least 60 days of measured false positives, false negatives, human reversals, and trademark performance.
